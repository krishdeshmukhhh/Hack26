# Interview and consent guidance

Human facilitator must complete these fields privately before inviting anyone:
responsible person/contact; study purpose; actual model/service and location;
which screenshots, audio or transcripts leave the laptop and retention by each
provider; local logs/storage and authorized viewers; deletion date (suggested
seven days after study); withdrawal deadline and procedure using study ID.
If processing routes or provider retention are unknown, resolve them before use.
Do not put consent forms or personal data in this repository.

Read: Participation is voluntary. This prototype may give incorrect advice.
We record task outcomes, time, observed actions and optional ratings/notes under
a study ID. Curio processes voice and screen content through [actual routes].
Use only the supplied files and clean desktop; close personal applications and
notifications. You can stop, skip questions or request deletion via [contact]
before [deadline]. Participation does not authorize publicity. Declining does
not affect your relationship with the team or institution.

Record separate affirmative choices privately: participation/notes yes/no;
necessary model processing yes/no. If either is no, do not begin. Optional
screen/audio recording, anonymous verbatim quotation and named attribution each
have separate yes/no choices, default no. No recording is needed for this pilot.
For any later publication obtain authorization for the actual material and use;
this workstream has no authorization to contact people or transmit study data.
A withdrawal may require deleting notes/assets as well as the CSV row. Do not
retain identifying withdrawal information in the repo. Follow institutional
requirements; this guidance is not a substitute for required review.

Before describing the prototype: Tell me about a recent time software
instructions did not match your screen. What did you try? What happened?

After all tasks (ask equally about either condition):

1. Walk me through where you became uncertain, if anywhere.
2. What helped you decide what to do next?
3. Did any instruction seem wrong or difficult to verify? What did you do?
4. What changed when you moved between the application and help?
5. Which resources would you choose for a similar task, and why? Neither is fine.
6. What, if anything, could you now do without help? What would still be hard?
7. Was there anything uncomfortable about voice or sharing the screen?
8. What have we missed?

Do not ask whether Curio made them faster/smarter or solicit praise. Probe
concrete events neutrally. Preserve exact consented quotations with private
provenance; distinguish impressions from observed outcomes, including criticism.
