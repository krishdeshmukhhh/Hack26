# Participant goal cards

Read after private consent: We are studying how people use desktop instructions.
We are evaluating the tools, not you. Advice may be wrong. You may skip a question
or stop at any time. Use only supplied practice files, never personal accounts.
Tell us when you consider the result finished. Each task has a ten-minute limit.

In the usual-resources task you may use Calc help, web search, guides and videos,
but no AI assistants or other people. In the Curio task use Curio for
help; do not open web/help resources. You may explore Calc freely in either.
In the final task use Calc only, with no assistant, help pages or researcher hints.
We will tell you which condition applies before each task.

## A ? High scores (reveal only at its start)

In the supplied workbook, make scores in B2:B7 strictly greater than 10 appear
with a light yellow background using conditional formatting. Preserve all data.
Save your work as A-result.ods in the provided study folder.

## B ? Held items (reveal only at its start)

In the supplied workbook, make status cells in C2:C7 equal to Hold appear with a
light yellow background using conditional formatting. Preserve all data.
Save your work as B-result.ods in the provided study folder.

## C ? Low scores (reveal after the break)

Without help, make scores in B2:B7 less than or equal to 10 appear with a light
yellow background using conditional formatting. Preserve all data.
Save your work as C-result.ods in the provided study folder.

After each task: How much effort did this task require (1 very low?7 very high)?
How frustrated did you feel (1 very low?7 very high)? You may leave either blank.
