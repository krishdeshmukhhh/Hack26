recorded walkthrough references and inspectable heuristic skill estimates can
inform later requests. These are source/test claims. A separate bounded
polynomial tutor independently checks supported equations.

**Unsupported claims:** first/unique or superior assistance; faster completion,
better retention/independence or lower anxiety; calibrated mastery or proven
cross-app learning; independent ordinary-GUI verification; crowd adoption from
seeded data; guaranteed anonymity; all-local inference; universal accessibility,
scam prevention or general autonomous execution.

task records and visible concept evidence. Show one desktop task, then its record
and changed assistance context. Live-model adherence and human benefit are
unmeasured. Copilot already offers screen-aware highlighting; Whatfix already
offers interactive flows and audience targeting.
